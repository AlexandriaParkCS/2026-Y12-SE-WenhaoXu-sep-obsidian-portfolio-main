There are already many existing alternative to learn fire safety. These tools include Sparky's Fun House, Home Fire Safety Hero, and educational videos or fire drills at home. While these platforms are useful for children, teaching them about what to do in a fire, finding the exit and a meeting place, they lack engaging gameplay that will retain this information in the case of an emergency. 

Fire Safety education will be a simple but engaging game, with feedback loops that can actually let children retain information. It will be accessible to people of all ages.
