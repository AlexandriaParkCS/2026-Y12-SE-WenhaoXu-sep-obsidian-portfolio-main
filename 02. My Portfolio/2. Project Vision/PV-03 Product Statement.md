For children and students who have harder times concentrating, and lack the knowledge of fire safety or need revision, Fire Safety Education is a simplistic, gamified and engaging tool that allows children learn about fire safety, and make fast decisions in the case of a real fire. 

Unlike other tools that are either too simple or too complex designed for children with little literacy or full on fire simulations, Fire Safety Education offers an accessible, simple and engaging tool tailored to children and students while still teaching them about fire safety. 

Fire Safety Education will require only a web browser and basic internet access, and will be independent and self-contained, providing an educational space where students will learn with the use of engaging game elements, immediate feedback loops and consequential learning. This would improve fire safety awareness among children while encouraging active participation and long-term learning retention.
