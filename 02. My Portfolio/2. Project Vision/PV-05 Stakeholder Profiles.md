**Primary Stakeholders**
**Students and Young Adults**
- **Description**: Individuals aged from 5 to 25 may encounter emergency situations at home, school or in other public environments
- **Needs:** Information about fire safety, and an engaging learning tool with feedback
- **Success Criteria**: Students demonstrate understanding of emergency procedures and apply it in the events of an emergency
**Educators**
- **Description:** Educators need a way to teach students about fire safety
* **Needs**: Accessible educational tools that can complement fire safety drills
- **Success Criteria**: Students demonstrate understanding of emergency procedures and apply it in the events of an emergency
