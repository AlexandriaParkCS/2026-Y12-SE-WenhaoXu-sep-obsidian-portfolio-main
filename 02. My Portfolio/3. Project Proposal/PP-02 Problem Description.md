The problem of young students struggling to retain and apply fire safety knowledge due to unengaging teaching methods, overly complex materials, and the lack of interactive, accessible educational tools affects students, educators, and families who rely on effective safety education to support children's wellbeing and emergency preparedness.

The impact of this problem is that children often fail to absorb critical fire safety procedures, struggle to recall key steps under pressure, and lack the confidence to act decisively in dangerous situations. This leads to poor emergency preparedness, reduced awareness, and potentially life-threatening gaps in knowledge. Existing fire safety resources are often too passive, dull, or inaccessible for young audiences, creating barriers for children who need engaging, memorable guidance.

A successful solution would be a potentially web-based, youth-friendly fire safety education application that uses gamification to place students in simulated emergency scenarios where they must make their own decisions. It would provide interactive learning experiences, clear visual guidance, and an intuitive interface that encourages repeated engagement. The solution would empower young students to build confident, practical responses to fire situations, improve their decision-making under pressure, and retain life-saving safety knowledge in a way that is both effective and enjoyable.
