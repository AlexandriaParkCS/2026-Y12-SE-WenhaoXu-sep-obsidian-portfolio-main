**Gameplay Experience**

The main gameplay screen is the core experience of Fire Safety Education. It is designed to be engaging, intuitive, and age-appropriate, immersing young students in realistic fire safety scenarios that encourage active decision-making.

The gameplay screen presents students with a simulated emergency scenario, accompanied by clear visuals and simple, readable text describing the situation. At each stage, the student is prompted to choose from a set of actions (e.g., "Call 000", "Use the nearest exit", "Check the doorknobs"). Each decision leads to a unique outcome, with immediate feedback explaining whether the choice was safe and why. This reinforces correct behaviour and gently corrects misconceptions in a low-pressure environment.

Progress through the scenario is tracked visually, giving students a sense of advancement and encouraging them to complete each module. Upon finishing a scenario, the student receives a summary of their decisions and a score reflecting how safely they responded.

The interface is intentionally colourful and visually driven to reduce reading barriers and maintain engagement for younger audiences. The visual hierarchy guides students naturally through each decision point, while consistent iconography and friendly design reinforce a safe, supportive learning environment.
